03 — The process

Four movements, one file.

  1. I

    Tell us the plan

    Country, purpose, dates, and any previous refusal. A free consultation is often enough to see the path. A paid detailed session is there when the file is more complex.

  2. II

    We map the documents

    You leave with a personalised checklist — what is essential, what can wait, and what usually causes delays. Lists are confirmed for your case, not copied from a generic blog.

  3. III

    You apply, prepared

    Forms, appointments and supporting papers, reviewed before they leave your desk. We stay reachable on call or WhatsApp during office hours.

  4. IV

    We stay until there is an answer

    Updates as the file moves. If the decision is a refusal, we can review the letter and discuss a fresh application or an appeal.
