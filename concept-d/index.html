FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee a visa?

No. Incredible Travellers is a private consultancy. Visa decisions are made only by the embassy or immigration authority. We help you prepare a clear, complete file — we cannot promise the outcome.

What is the difference between the free and paid consultation?

The free consultation is a first conversation about your plan. The paid detailed consultation is a longer sitting: a review of your documents, profile and any refusal history, ending with a personalised checklist. The fee is ₹[to confirm] and the duration is [to confirm].

What documents will I need?

It depends on the country and the visa type. We confirm a list for your case during the consultation, rather than handing out a generic checklist.

How long does a visa take?

Processing times vary by country, season and visa type, and are set by the authorities. We will share the latest typical range we are seeing, marked [to confirm] until we check it for your file.

Can you help after a refusal?

Yes. We can read the refusal letter with you and discuss a fresh application or an appeal, where that is possible.

Are you a government office?

No. We are a private visa and travel consultancy in Delhi. We are not affiliated with any embassy or immigration authority.
