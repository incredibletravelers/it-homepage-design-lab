How long does a visa take?

It depends on the country, visa type and season. We share current official processing times for your case at the first consultation and keep you updated after you apply.

What documents will I need?

Usually a valid passport, photos, financial proof, and travel or study details. You’ll get a personal checklist written for your profile — not a generic list.

Do you guarantee approval?

No — and nobody honestly can. Decisions are made only by the embassy or immigration authority. What we guarantee is a complete, carefully reviewed application.

Can you help with USA visa appointments?

Yes. We help with the DS-160, scheduling and interview preparation. Appointment availability is set by the US embassy and can change.

How do I pay your fees?

After your consultation you receive a written quote and secure payment options. We never ask for payment through unofficial channels. [payment methods to confirm]
